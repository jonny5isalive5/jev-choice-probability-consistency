## What the prompts actually elicited

Jev chose HEADS in all 40 coin requests, including both option orders, with HEADS probabilities ranging from 0.58 to 0.73 and confidence 0.16 to 0.46. Thus a question about an unseen fair coin did not produce the near-equal scores hoped for in this experiment. This is an observation about the model's outputs, not an observed series of coin tosses. There is no known individual physical outcome and no answer-accuracy score.

The spinner yielded RED 34 times, GREEN 25 and BLUE once. Confidence ranged from 0.01 to 0.12. Thirteen responses had a top-two margin <=0.01; five had a reported full-vector spread <=0.02. Neither statistic reveals the pre-correction rounded vector. All six ternary orders were retained equally.

## Corrected design and checks

The original proposed spinner falsifier was removed. A non-selected reported 0.34 in a 0.34/0.33/0.33 vector can be generated by the correction algorithm and is not automatically a contradiction. The new checker enumerates all permissible hundredth residuals and solves for a latent normalized vector with the selected option maximal, using exact rational arithmetic. Strict-interior witnesses avoid assumptions about the unknown nearest-rounding tie convention; boundary-only candidates would be unresolved.

The collector was separately revised for both two- and three-option confidence formulas. It validates each response against that request's options, preserves raw bytes before validation, and continues on hypothesis contradictions. Structural, transport, model and billing stops remained active. Seven offline tests passed, including 500 simulated forward examples, false-refutation regression, known incompatibilities, schedule balance, response retention and budget checks. Protocol, payloads, schedule, collector, solver and analyzer were frozen before the first live call.

No change was made to earlier adapters, evidence, thresholds or reports. Five previously saved 0.99 totals remain counterexamples to an unconditional always-normalizes account of the entire history. This new compatible-but-inconclusive run does not erase them or make the earlier ranking discrepancies harmless. Canonical-field semantics still need provider confirmation.
